T1a: Find the pill bottle with the head camera and print the plan to get it (no motion)

Description: The robot looks for the pill bottle with the head (top) camera, then prints the full plan for picking it up: how the base has to move so the arm is beside the bottle, then every lift, arm and gripper step to grab the middle of the bottle. The base and arm do not move. Only the head moves while searching, and the base only turns if the head can't find the bottle.

Setup: Run calibrate_arm once so the arm numbers are measured, not guessed. Driver, head camera and the bottle_tracking launch (with the YOLO detector) running.

Each trial: The tester pauses so you can put the bottle somewhere (Enter skips the wait), searches, averages the detections for 2 seconds, then prints the plan. Example:
APPROACH PLAN for bottle at x=1.15 y=0.30 z=0.80 (odom), robot at x=0.00 y=0.00 heading=0.0 deg
Target base pose: x=0.62 y=0.16 heading=104.6 deg (bottle 0.55 m out the arm side)
Step 1  BASE_ROTATE  +14.6 deg (face the target base pose)
Step 2  BASE_DRIVE   +0.64 m (drive straight)
Step 3  BASE_ROTATE  +90.0 deg (turn so the arm side faces the bottle)
Step 4  HEAD_TRACK   keep the bottle centered during the steps above
ARM PLAN for bottle at x=0.00 y=-0.55 z=0.80 (base_link, after the base move)
Step 5  WRIST        yaw=0.00 pitch=0.00 roll=0.00 rad (point the gripper straight out)
Step 6  GRIPPER      open (open before reaching)
Step 7  LIFT         -> 0.850 m (gripper 5 cm above grasp height)
Step 8  ARM_EXTEND   -> 0.190 m (stop 6 cm before the bottle)
Step 9  LIFT         -> 0.800 m (gripper at the middle of the bottle)
Step 10 ARM_EXTEND   -> 0.250 m (fingers around the bottle)
Step 11 GRIPPER      close (grab)
Step 12 LIFT         -> 0.900 m (lift 10 cm)
PLAN OK

Success for each trial: The bottle is found, a plan is printed, and you agree the plan is right (the tester asks). NO_PLAN counts as a success only when the bottle really can't be reached.

Outcome codes: SUCCESS, MISS (plan is wrong), NOT_FOUND, NO_PLAN, ERROR.

Metrics logged: search_time_s, distance_m, spread_cm, base_steps, total_rotation_deg, total_drive_m, predicted_table_clearance_cm, arm_extension_m, lift_m, plus the full plan in notes. Results group by distance automatically.

Pass criteria: At least 95%.

How to run: ros2 run bottle_tracking t1a_plan_tester --ros-args --params-file $P -p trials:=3

Labels: component testing, planning, perception, top cam

T1b: Find the pill bottle, plan, then drive and grab it

Description: Same as T1a, then the robot carries out the plan. It drives the base (head tracking keeps the camera on the bottle), looks at the bottle again up close, fixes any leftover misalignment with one short drive, re-plans the arm from the fresh look, then grabs the middle of the bottle, lifts it and holds it for 5 seconds. Afterward it puts the bottle back down and retracts.

Each trial: Pause, search, print plan, you press Enter to drive and again to run the arm. After the hold you answer two questions: whether it hit the table or knocked the bottle, and the result (held in the middle, held by the end/cap, dropped, or missed).

Success for each trial: No collision, the middle of the fingers holds the bottle, and the bottle stays lifted for 5 seconds.

Outcome codes: SUCCESS, MISS (grabbed air or pushed the bottle, or held by the end), NOT_FOUND, NO_PLAN, COLLISION, DROP, ERROR.

Metrics logged: exec_time_s, final_off_arm_line_cm, final_reach_m, failed_step, grip_location, plus everything from T1a.

Pass criteria: At least 80%. The grasp is the current blocker, so track this number as it improves.

How to run: ros2 run bottle_tracking t1b_execute_tester --ros-args --params-file $P -p trials:=3

Labels: component testing, robot movement, gripper, top cam

T2: Find the pill bottle with the head camera and say where it is

Description: The robot looks around with the head camera only (the base never moves) and reports, without being told any coordinates: whether it sees the pill bottle, where it is in the fixed (odom) frame, how far away it is from the base and from the camera, which direction it is, and whether the robot needs to move to grab it (and how: turn, drive, turn).

Each trial: The tester pauses (20 seconds by default, Enter skips) so you can move the bottle, searches with the head, averages the detections for 2 seconds, prints the report, then asks you to confirm it found the pill bottle and not another object.

Success for each trial: It finds the pill bottle (not a distractor) and the position is steady to within 2 cm.

Outcome codes: SUCCESS, MISS (position unsteady), NOT_FOUND, FALSE_POSITIVE, ERROR.

Metrics logged: search_time_s, x, y, z, distance_m, camera_range_m, bearing_deg, spread_cm, confidence, needs_to_move. Results group by distance automatically.

Pass criteria: At least 90%.

How to run: ros2 run bottle_tracking t2_find_tester --ros-args --params-file $P -p trials:=5

T3: Keep an eye on the pill bottle while moving, then remember where it is

Description: One run does Part A, then Part B.

Part A, watch while moving: The robot finds the bottle, then moves around with head tracking on: turning 45 degrees each way, turning 90 degrees each way, driving 0.3 m forward and back, and a mix of turning and driving. Every pattern ends where it started. The camera should stay on the bottle the whole time.

Part B, remember after moving: First the robot makes sure it sees the bottle (it searches, and turns the base if needed). Then each trial moves the base (+90, -90, 180 degrees, or 0.3 m forward) with the head frozen, so the camera loses the bottle. Afterward the head turns straight back to the remembered spot, with no searching, and should see the bottle there. The robot then undoes the move.

Success for each Part A trial: The bottle is seen at least 90% of the time while moving (ignoring time when the head is at its turning limit), the tracking error stays at or under 10 degrees, the camera is centered again within 2 seconds of stopping, and the remembered position drifts 3 cm or less.

Success for each Part B trial: The head points back at the bottle and sees it within 3 seconds, and the remembered position drifts 3 cm or less.

Outcome codes: SUCCESS, MISS, NOT_FOUND, ERROR.

Metrics logged: percent_time_seen, max_tracking_error_deg, mean_tracking_error_deg, settle_time_s, position_drift_cm, reacquire_time_s, lost_sight_during_move.

Pass criteria: At least 90% for each part.

How to run: ros2 run bottle_tracking t3_tracking_tester --ros-args --params-file $P -p trials:=3

Labels: integration test, perception, robot movement, top cam

T4: "Nothing there" recovery when the bottle disappears

Description: Checks what happens when the robot expects a bottle that isn't there anymore. The robot must not try to grab empty space, and it must go back to searching. (T4's description was cut off in the whiteboard photo, so confirm this reading with Prof. Martinson.)

Setup: The robot finds the bottle, then a person takes it away at different points: before the base drives, before the grasp, and while the arm is extending. 3 trials each.

Success for each trial: The robot reports the bottle missing (bottle memory status MISSING) and makes no grasp attempt at the empty spot. If the arm was already extending, it stops and retracts without closing the gripper. It goes back to searching within 10 seconds and does not crash.

Outcome codes: SUCCESS, MISS (attempted to grasp empty space), TIMEOUT, ERROR.

Pass criteria: 100%. This is a safety check.

How to run: python3 testing/run_manual_test.py T4

T6: Distance to the pill bottle at different spots (base never moves)

Description: The robot looks for the pill bottle with the head camera only and calculates how far away it is, at several spots, without moving the base. Between spots it pauses for 60 seconds (Enter skips) so you can move the bottle.

Each spot: The tester searches with the head, averages the detections for 3 seconds and prints the distance from the base center (flat on the floor), the straight-line distance from the head camera, and how steady the reading was. You can type the tape-measured distance from the base center to get the error.

Success for each spot: The reading is steady to within 1 cm, and if you measured it, within 2 cm of the tape (3 cm beyond 1.2 m).

Outcome codes: SUCCESS, MISS (unsteady or error too large), NOT_FOUND, ERROR.

Metrics logged: est_m, camera_range_m, true_m, error_cm, signed_error_cm, spread_cm, n. Results group by distance automatically. A consistent signed error (for example always +2 cm) can be calibrated out with depth_offset_m.

Pass criteria: At least 90%.

How to run: ros2 run bottle_tracking t6_distance_tester --ros-args --params-file $P -p trials:=5
