Pill Bottle Node Map

ROS 2 Jazzy layout for the Stretch 2 pick-and-verify demo. Each existing script becomes one node, and every node can be started on its own with a tester so perception, distance, gripper vision, planning and motion can be checked separately.

Component testing strategy

For each tester, start the robot (ros2 launch pill_bottle_nodes robot.launch.py), then the nodes it needs (ros2 launch pill_bottle_nodes nodes.launch.py stage:=…), then the tester (ros2 run pill_bottle_nodes test_…). Run the testers top to bottom. Each one assumes the ones above it pass, so when something breaks, the first tester that fails points to the component at fault. Pass targets are starting values to tighten after the first measurements.

TesterWhat the component doesHow to testExpected outcome (pass)Robot
test_cameras
stage:=cameras		 Streams head color + aligned depth and the claw's mono image Launch, check rates with ros2 topic hz, save one frame from each camera Head color and depth at 10 Hz or more, claw at 2 Hz or more; head frame upright after rotation; claw frame shows the fingertips still
test_detector
stage:=detector		 Finds pill_bottle, cap and label boxes in the head image Bottle on table and floor at 0.5, 1.0 and 1.5 m; 10 frames each; saves annotated images Bottle found in at least 9 of 10 frames at each distance, confidence 0.4 or higher, box on the bottle still
test_distance
stage:=distance		 Turns a box + depth into x, y, z from the robot base and labels the bottle upright, tilted or lying Place the bottle on 5 tape-measured spots, in each orientation Position within 2 cm up to 1 m (4 cm at 1.5 m); orientation label correct every time still
test_gripper_vision
stage:=gripper_vision		 Finds the bottle in the claw image and reports its center offset and orientation Hold the bottle centered between the fingertips, then 3 cm left and 3 cm right Bottle seen in all three; offset sign matches the direction it was moved still
test_planner
stage:=planner		 Converts the bottle pose into lift, arm and wrist targets, or a base move if out of reach Dry run on a bottle that is close, at the edge of reach, and out of reach In reach: targets inside limits (lift 0.05–1.05 m, arm 0–0.50 m, wrist ±0.6 rad). Out of reach: a drive of 1 m or less. Nothing moves still
test_motion
stage:=motion		 Sends every move to the Stretch driver and refuses unsafe ones Asks for an out-of-range lift (must be refused), then nudges each joint 2 cm or 0.2 rad and back Unsafe goal refused; each joint arrives within 1 cm (lift, arm) or 0.05 rad (wrist, head) a little
test_gripper
stage:=gripper		 The claw by itself: how far it closes empty, on the bottle, and whether it holds Close empty, close on a bottle held between the pads, then let go and watch the finger for 8 s Grip effort on the bottle clearly above empty (measured: 6.6 vs 0.1); still holding after 8 s with at least half the closing effort; prints grip_effort_min claw only
test_arm
stage:=arm		 Moves the claw to the pregrasp point, 10 cm short of the bottle Confirm before each move, hand on the runstop; measure the final gap Claw stops 10 cm (±2 cm) from the bottle, stays at least 3.5 cm above the table, no contact arm
test_base
stage:=base		 Turns in place in 60° steps and drives up to 1 m toward the bottle Tape marks on the floor; command one turn and one 0.5 m drive Turn within ±5°, drive within ±3 cm; wrist is stowed before the base moves base
test_grasp
stage:=grasp		 Only the grasp: approach, claw-camera refine, close, lift 5 cm and watch the grip, put it back Bottle upright on the table, 5+ runs; change one setting at a time and compare with grasp_report Result is HELD (not SLIPPING, DROPPED or MISSED); each run logged with the claw image from just before closing arm + base
test_task
stage:=full		 The task manager's search and planning, with nothing moving Dry run from where the robot stands; then task_manager -p stop_after:=grasp, stop_after:=verify Finds the bottle and prints a reach plan; later stages stop where asked still
task_manager
stage:=full		 Search, approach, reach, refine with claw camera, grasp, verify, place Bottle upright, knocked over, and elevated; one deliberate miss Bottle held up to the head camera for 8 s and detected, then set down; after a miss it retries, stopping after 4 attempts arm + base
